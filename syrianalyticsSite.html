About Us

Clarity in a
complex world

Syrianalytics is Syria's pioneering data analytics and business intelligence company — purpose-built to serve small and medium-sized enterprises that have historically lacked access to professional analytics tools and expertise.

We believe every Syrian business deserves the same quality of data insight that powers global enterprises — at a price point and pace that actually works for them. From dashboard visualization to predictive modeling, our solutions are tailored, affordable, and built for real impact.

Beyond delivering solutions, we invest in building Syria's data talent. Our hands-on training programs in Excel, Tableau, Power BI, Python, and R programming equip businesses and professionals with the skills to own their own analytical capabilities.
